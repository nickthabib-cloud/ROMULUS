- agent mode off;
- the same window, 2024-11-18 10:00–17:30 UTC;
- the same sources: host and network indices, Arkime sessions, and the packet stage;
- pack `m3-pack@2`;
- gold v1;
- the same adjudication set. Run 10 adds two `benign` verdicts.

- **Neither hits any of the 4 discovery opportunities that are still misses.** The identity check it found belongs to the root interactive-shell opportunity, not to a separate discovery opportunity. No expansion link reached any record of the 4 missed discovery steps (see below).

**Correct groupings.** Two multi-finding incidents each group one attack-chain segment correctly. Every member is a Tier A hit, on two consecutive opportunities:

### Are the reworded SSH-login claims right?

**Yes for the login counts.**

- **Attacker:** "at least 1 login". The raw data show exactly 1, so run 9's overcount of 2 is fixed.
- **Lab account:** "at least 15 logins". The raw data show 15 sshd `Accepted` records and 15 distinct audit sessions. The 10 USER_LOGIN records the claim lists are accurate: they are successful terminal logins in 5 of those sessions. The 15 failed pre-authentication USER_LOGIN records are correctly not counted.

**Two minor issues remain on the lab item:**

- it cites 20 of the 25 records it counts;
- its claim range ends about 20 min after the last cited record.

### Do the 2 unrebuilt packet sessions touch gold evidence?

**No.**

- Neither is a gold locator, and no gold opportunity matches either one on entity and time within ±10 min.
- The hunt's `partial` state therefore hides nothing from gold.

**The one request-only session is different.** The packet stage lists it as a note, not a partial reason, but it **is** gold `action` evidence of the exploitation step. That step is already Reported from host evidence, and the packet rule judges responses only.

## Why expansion could not reach the post-login discovery steps

All 44 `login_session` links are unsupported. The brief attributes this to www-data and cron anchors with an unset audit session. That is true for 16 of the 22 anchors: 14 www-data and 2 network-only. **It is not true for the other 6.**

**What is wrong.** Those 6 root anchors sit in two real audit sessions: one cron-launched and one the attacker's SSH login. Their raw auditd records carry the session, but the stored typed events they cite do not.

**Cause.**

- Those events were first stored by earlier hunts, before session extraction was added to the audit normalizer.
- The normalizer string stayed `lab-ecs@1`.
- The store keeps the first version of a typed event (ignore on conflict).
- Expansion builds its anchors from stored rows.

Replaying today's normalizer over the same raw records gives the session ID, under the same event IDs.

**Effects:**

- Expansion results depend on store history.
- A determinism check on the same store cannot detect this.

**Counterfactual** (custodian replay, not ROMULUS output). With fresh session keys, the SSH-session anchors' session links would cover the records of all 4 missed discovery steps and of the remote-login entry. Under today's rules:

- only 1 of the 4 discovery steps uses a command on the post-access discovery rule's list;
- the remote-login entry would still not be promoted. The session's only session-keyed sshd success record is a PAM session-open, which the promotion rule skips by design, and this non-interactive session has no USER_LOGIN record.

Fixing the stale rows alone would therefore add at most one reported opportunity (discovery, low).

## Anomalies

There are 3 locator-match anomalies, the same 3 as in run 9. All are the known limitation in PROTOCOL §13: the inferable-only tool-transfer opportunity's indirect evidence lies outside its window. None is a hit, and none changes a verdict.

## Caveats

- One run per arm, n = 22 observable opportunities. The intervals are wide.
- **Expansion budget.** Expansion ran under its default budget and stopped on it. All 22 root anchors are `expanded_partial`, and the 20% reserve for discovered anchors (36 queries) went unspent because no anchor was discovered. Run 10 measures expansion at this budget and with this store, not expansion in general.
- **Review order.** Neither hunt has a report-manifest revision, so the replicated manifest sort (lane, −priority, ts) is used. Within a priority, an earlier event ranks first. That is why the boot-time false lead (10:21 UTC) ranks above high-lane true positives of the same priority.
- **Scoring copy.** Scored from a VM `/tmp` copy of the committed `eval/` (`6eea132`). No harness change was needed: expansion items are ordinary candidates, and expansion tasks are `task` rows the funnel already handles.
