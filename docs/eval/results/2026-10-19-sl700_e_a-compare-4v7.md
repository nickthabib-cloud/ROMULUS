What `m2-pack@1` adds:

- the `net_socket` family, built from auditd connect/SOCKADDR records;

Aggregate and class level only.

## Reading

**What the new rules added**

- **Both c2_channel opportunities are now surfaced, and one is reported.** These were the misses that stopped at *retrieved*, because auditd connect records were not normalized before.
- These two are the only opportunities that differ between the runs. Run 7 hits nothing new in any other class and loses nothing.
- Recall reported goes from 11/22 to 12/22, and critical+high reported from 9/13 to 10/13.
- Recall surfaced goes from 15/22 to 17/22, and critical+high surfaced from 10/13 to 12/13.
- The other c2_channel opportunity reached the lead lane only.

**What they cost**

- The two new rules surfaced 32 items: 6 Tier A hits and 26 adjudicated benign.
  - `host.interpreter_external_connection`: 25 surfaced (3 Tier A, 22 benign).
  - `corr.process_network_link`: 7 surfaced (3 Tier A, 4 benign).
- The benign items are ZoneMinder perl at boot contacting external HTTPS (telemetry and IP lookup), and root `python3.10` on the firewall and DNS hosts contacting Ubuntu/Canonical archive addresses and public DNS (update and motd traffic).
- False leads rise from 41 to 67, and false leads per review hour (modeled at 5 min per item) from 5.07 to 6.23.
- P@10 drops from 1.00 to 0.80. The top 10 now holds 2 benign high-lane `host.interpreter_external_connection` items (the boot-time perl connections). The top 20 also holds 1 benign high-lane `corr.process_network_link` item, and P@20 stays at 0.85.

**Unchanged and other effects**

- Citation checks pass on every reported item: 22/22, with 138/138 citations retained and matching the source digest.
- Time to first reported item falls to 3.1 s, because the new rule fires on boot-time traffic early in the window. That is a false lead, not an actionable finding. Time to first actionable finding is 54.9 s.

## Caveats

- One run per arm, and n = 22 observable opportunities, so the Wilson intervals are wide.
- **Review order:** run 7 has no report-manifest revision, so its review order is the replicated manifest sort (same key).
- **VM state:** the VM reported `b8be0dc` at scoring time, not `7657c65`; I did not deploy. Scoring used the hunt's stored records. The funnel's normalizer replay used the deployed venv, which normalizes `net_socket`.
